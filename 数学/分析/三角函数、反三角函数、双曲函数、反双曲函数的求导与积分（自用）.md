在这篇文章中集中计算一下最简单的六个三角函数  `!$\sin ,\cos ,\tan ,\csc ,\sec ,\cot $`  六个反三角函数  `!$\arcsin ,\arccos ,\arctan ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t}$`  六个双曲函数  `!$\sinh ,\cosh, \tanh, \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h}  $`  和六个反双曲函数  `!$\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r}  \mathrm{s} \mathrm{i} \mathrm{n} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r}  \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{n} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c}  \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r}  \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} ,\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r}  \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h}  $` 的导函数和原函数。以便未来查阅，防止遗忘。

## 三角函数

### 三角函数的导函数

(1)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sin x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} x} \sin x &=\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin \left( x+\Delta x\right)  -\sin x}{\Delta x} \\ &=\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin x\cos \Delta x+\cos x\sin \Delta x-\sin x}{\Delta x} \\ &=\sin x\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\cos \Delta x-1}{\Delta x} +\cos x\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin \Delta x}{\Delta x} \\ &=0\cdot \sin x+\cos x\\ &=\cos x \end{align} $` 

(2)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cos x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} x} \cos x &=\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\cos \left( x+\Delta x\right)  -\cos x}{\Delta x} \\ &=\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\cos x\cos \Delta x-\sin x\sin \Delta x-\cos x}{\Delta x} \\ &=\cos x\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\cos \Delta x-1}{\Delta x} -\sin x\lim_{\Delta x\rightarrow 0} \frac{\sin \Delta x}{\Delta x} \\ &=0\cdot \cos x-\sin x\\ &=-\sin x \end{align} $` 

(3)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\tan x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \tan x &=\frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \left( \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \right)  \\ &=\frac{1}{\cos^{2} x}  \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \cos x-\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \sin x \right)\\ &=\frac{\cos^{2} x+\sin^{2} x}{\cos^{2} x} \\ &=\sec^{2} x \end{align} $` 

(4)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\csc x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \csc x &=\frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \frac{1}{\sin x} \\ &=\frac{1}{\sin^{2} x} \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( 1\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \sin x-\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \cdot 1\right)  \\ &=-\frac{\cos x}{\sin^{2} x} \\ &=-\csc x\cot x \end{align} $` 

(5)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sec x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \sec x &=\frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \frac{1}{\cos x} \\ &=\frac{1}{\cos^{2} x} \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( 1\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \cos x-\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \cdot 1\right)  \\ &=\frac{\sin x}{\cos^{2} x} \\ &=\sec x\tan x \end{align} $` 

(6)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cot x$`  的导函数

 `!$ \begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \cot x &=\frac{\mathrm{d} }{\mathrm{d} x} \left( \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} \right)  \\ &=\frac{1}{\sin^{2} x}  \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \sin x-\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \cos x \right)\\ &=-\frac{\cos^{2} x+\sin^{2} x}{\sin^{2} x} \\ &=-\csc^{2} x \end{align} $` 

### 三角函数的原函数

(7)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sin x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \sin x\mathrm{d} x &=-\int \left( -\sin x\right)  \mathrm{d} x\\ &=-\cos x+C \end{align} $` 

(8)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cos x$`  的原函数

 `!$\displaystyle\int \cos x\mathrm{d} x=\sin x+C $` 

(9)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\tan x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \tan x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=-\int \frac{-\sin x}{\cos x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=-\int \frac{1}{\cos x} \mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  \\ &=-\ln \left\vert \cos x\right\vert  +C \end{align}$` 

(10)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\csc x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \csc x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{-\sin x}{-\sin^{2} x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  }{\cos^{2} x-1} \\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  }{\left( \cos x+1\right)  \left( \cos x-1\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \left( \frac{1}{\cos x-1} -\frac{1}{\cos x+1} \right)  \mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x-1\right)}{\cos x-1}   -\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x+1\right)}{\cos x+1}   \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \cos x-1\right\vert  -\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \cos x+1\right\vert  +C\\ &=\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{\cos x-1}{\cos x+1} \right\vert  +C  \end{align}$` 

除此之外，也有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \csc x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{1}{\sin x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{1}{\sin \frac{x}{2} \cos \frac{x}{2} } \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\cos \frac{x}{2} }{\sin \frac{x}{2} } \cdot \sec^{2} \frac{x}{2} \mathrm{d} \left( \frac{x}{2} \right)  \\ &=\int \frac{1}{\tan \frac{x}{2} } \mathrm{d} \left( \tan \frac{x}{2} \right)  \\ &=\ln \left\vert \tan \frac{x}{2} \right\vert  +C  \end{align}$` 

还有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \csc x\mathrm{d} x &=-\int \frac{\csc x\left( -\csc x-\cot x\right)  }{\csc x+\cot x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=-\int \frac{-\csc^{2} x-\csc x\cot x}{\csc x+\cot x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=-\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cot x+\csc x\right)  }{\csc x+\cot x} \\ &=-\ln \left| \csc x+\cot x\right|  +C \end{align}$` 

以上三种结果是等价的，这是因为

 `!$\displaystyle \frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{\cos x-1}{\cos x+1} \right\vert  =\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{2\sin^{2} \frac{x}{2} }{2\cos^{2} \frac{x}{2} } \right\vert  =\ln \left| \tan \frac{x}{2} \right|   $` 

和

 `!$\displaystyle -\ln \left| \csc x+\cot x\right|  =-\ln \left| \frac{1+\cos x}{\sin x} \right|  =\ln \left| \frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x} \right|  =\ln \left| \tan \frac{x}{2} \right|   $` 

(11)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sec x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \sec x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\cos x}{\cos^{2} x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{1-\sin^{2} x} \\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{\left( 1+\sin x\right)  \left( 1-\sin x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \left( \frac{1}{1-\sin x} +\frac{1}{1+\sin x} \right)  \mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  \\ &=-\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{ \mathrm{d} \left( 1-\sin x\right)}{1-\sin x}  +\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{ \mathrm{d} \left( 1+\sin x\right) }{1+\sin x} \\ &=-\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert 1-\sin x\right\vert  +\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert 1+\sin x\right\vert  +C\\ &=\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x} \right\vert  +C  \end{align}$` 

除此之外，也有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \sec x\mathrm{d} x&= \int \frac{1}{\cos x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{1}{\sin \left( \frac{\pi }{2} +x\right)  } \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \csc \left( x+\frac{\pi }{2} \right)  \mathrm{d} \left( x+\frac{\pi }{2} \right)  \\ &=\ln \left| \tan \left( \frac{x}{2} +\frac{\pi }{4} \right)  \right|  +C  \end{align}$` 

还有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \sec x\mathrm{d} x&= \int \frac{\sec x\left( \sec x+\tan x\right)  }{\sec x+\tan x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\sec^{2} x+\sec x\tan x}{\sec x+\tan x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \tan x+\sec x\right)  }{\sec x+\tan x} \\ &=\ln \left| \sec x+\tan x\right|  +C \end{align}$` 

以上三种结果是等价的，这是因为

 `!$\displaystyle \ln \left| \sec x+\tan x\right|  =\ln \left| \frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x} \right|  =\ln \left| \frac{1+\cos \left( \frac{\pi }{2} +x\right)  }{\sin \left( \frac{\pi }{2} +x\right)  } \right|  =\ln \left| \tan \left( \frac{x}{2} +\frac{\pi }{4} \right)  \right|   $` 

和

 `!$\displaystyle \frac{1}{2} \ln \left| \frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x} \right|  =\frac{1}{2} \ln \left| \frac{\left( 1+\sin x\right)^{2}  }{1-\sin^{2} x} \right|  =\frac{1}{2} \times 2\ln \left| \frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x} \right|  =\ln \left| \sec x+\tan x\right|   $` 

(12)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cot x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \cot x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin x\right)  }{\sin x} \\ &=\ln \left| \sin x\right|  +C \end{align}$` 

## 反三角函数

### 反三角函数的导函数

(13)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arcsin x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arcsin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arcsin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin \arcsin x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sin \arcsin x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \arcsin x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{\cos \arcsin x} \\ &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin^{2} \arcsin x} } \\ &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}} }   \end{align}$` 

(14)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arccos x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arccos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arccos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos \arccos x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos \arccos x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \cos x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{-\sin \arccos x} \\ &=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\cos^{2} \arccos x} } \\ &=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}} }  \end{align}$` 

(15)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arctan x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arctan x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \arctan x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \tan \arctan x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \tan \arctan x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \arctan x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{1}{\cos^{2} \arctan x} } \\ &=\cos^{2} \arctan x\\ &=\frac{1}{1+\tan^{2} \arctan x} \\ &=\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}   \end{align}$` 

(16)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c}\ x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \csc \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \csc \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{-\left| \csc \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right|  \cdot \cot \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x} \\ &=-\frac{1}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{\csc^{2} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x-1} } \\ &=-\frac{1}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{x^{2}-1} }   \end{align}$` 

(17)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c}\ x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \sec \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sec \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e}\mathrm{c} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{\left| \sec \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right|  \cdot \tan \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x} \\ &=\frac{1}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{\sec^{2} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-1} } \\ &=\frac{1}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{x^{2}-1} }  \end{align}$` 

(18)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t}\ x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \cot \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cot \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\right)  } } \\ &=\frac{1}{-\frac{1}{\sin^{2} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x} } \\ &=-\sin^{2} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x\\ &=-\frac{1}{1+\cot^{2} \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \  x} \\ &=-\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}   \end{align}$` 

### 反三角函数的原函数

(19)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arcsin x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \arcsin x\mathrm{d} x &=x\arcsin x-\int x\mathrm{d} \left( \arcsin x\right)  \\ &=x\arcsin x-\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}} } \mathrm{d} x\\ &=x\arcsin x-\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( x^{2}\right)  }{\sqrt{1-x^{{}2}} } \\ &=x\arcsin x+\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( 1-x^{2}\right)  }{2\sqrt{1-x^{2}} } \\ &=x\arcsin x+\sqrt{1-x^{2}} +C \end{align}$` 

(20)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arccos x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \arccos x\mathrm{d} x &=x\arccos x-\int x\mathrm{d} \left( \arccos x\right)  \\ &=x\arccos x-\int \left( -\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}} } \right)  \mathrm{d} x\\ &=x\arccos x+\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( x^{2}\right)  }{\sqrt{1-x^{2}} } \\ &=x\arccos x-\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( 1-x^{2}\right)  }{2\sqrt{1-x^{2}} } \\ &=x\arccos x-\sqrt{1-x^{2}} +C \end{align}$` 

(21)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\arctan x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \arctan x\mathrm{d} x &=x\arctan x-\int x\mathrm{d} \left( \arctan x\right)  \\ &=x\arctan x-\int \frac{x}{1+x^{2}} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=x\arctan x-\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{2x\mathrm{d} x}{1+x^{2}} \\ &=x\arctan x-\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( 1+x^{2}\right)  }{1+x^{2}} \\ &=x\arctan x-\frac{1}{2} \ln \left( 1+x^{2}\right)  +C \end{align}$` 

(22)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c}\ x$`  的原函数^[[计算中用到了这个结论](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/599964706)]

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\mathrm{d} x &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x-\int x\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  \\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x-\int \left( -\frac{x}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{x^{2}-1} } \right)  \mathrm{d} x\\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x+\mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{x^{2}-1} } \\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x+\mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\cdot \mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\ln \left( \left| x\right|  +\sqrt{x^{2}-1} \right)  +C\\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \  x+\ln \left( \left| x\right|  +\sqrt{x^{2}-1} \right)  +C \end{align}$` 

(23)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c}\ x$`  的原函数^[[计算中用到了这个结论](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/599964706)]

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\mathrm{d} x &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-\int x\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x\right)  \\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-\int \frac{x}{\left| x\right|  \sqrt{x^{2}-1} }   \mathrm{d} x\\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-\mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\int \frac{\mathrm{d} x}{\sqrt{x^{2}-1} } \\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-\mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\cdot \mathrm{s} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{n} \  x\ln \left( \left| x\right|  +\sqrt{x^{2}-1} \right)  +C\\ &=x\  \mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \  x-\ln \left( \left| x\right|  +\sqrt{x^{2}-1} \right)  +C \end{align}$` 

(24)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{a} \mathrm{r} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t}\ x$`  的原函数

## 双曲函数

### 双曲函数的导函数

(25)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sinh x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \sinh x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2} \right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( e^{x}\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} -\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( e^{-x}\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \right)  \\ &=\frac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2} \\ &=\cosh x \end{align}$` 

(26)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cosh x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \frac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2} \right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( e^{x}\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} +\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( e^{-x}\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \right)  \\ &=\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2} \\ &=\sinh x \end{align}$` 

(27)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\tanh x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \tanh x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x} \right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \\ &=\frac{\cosh^{2} x-\sinh^{2} x}{\cosh^{2} x} \\ &=\frac{1}{\cosh^{2} x}\\ &=\mathrm{sech}^{2}\ x  \end{align}$` 

(28)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{csch}\ x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \frac{1}{\sinh x} \right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \\ &=\frac{-\cosh x}{\sinh^{2} x} \\ &=-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\  \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x \end{align}$` 

(29)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{sech}\ x$`  的导函数

 `!$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{d} x}  &=\frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \frac{1}{\cosh x} \right)  }{\mathrm{d} x} \\ &=\frac{-\sinh x}{\cosh^{2} x} \\ &=-\mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\tanh x \end{align}$` 

(30)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{coth}\ x$`  的导函数

### 双曲函数的原函数

(31)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\sinh x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \sinh x\mathrm{d} x &=\cosh x+C\\  &=\frac{e^{x}+e^{-x}}{2} +C \end{align}$` 

(32)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\cosh x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \cosh x\mathrm{d} x &=\sinh x+C\\ &=\frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{2} +C \end{align}$` 

(33)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\tanh x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \tanh \mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x\right)  }{\cosh x} \\ &=\ln \left| \cosh x\right|  +C\\ &=\ln \left| e^{2x}+1\right| -x +C \end{align}$` 

(34)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{csch}\  x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{csch}\  x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\sinh x}{\sinh^{2} x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x\right)  }{\cosh^{2} x-1} \\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x\right)  }{\left( \cosh x+1\right)  \left( \cosh x-1\right)  } \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \left( \frac{1}{\cosh x-1} -\frac{1}{\cosh x+1} \right)  \mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x\right)  \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x-1\right)}{\cosh x-1}   -\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \cosh x+1\right)}{\cosh x+1}   \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \cosh x-1\right\vert  -\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \cosh x+1\right\vert  +C\\ &=\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{\cosh x-1}{\cosh x+1} \right\vert  +C  \end{align}$` 

除此之外，也有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{csch}\  x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{1}{\sinh x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\frac{1}{2} \int \frac{1}{\sinh \frac{x}{2} \cosh \frac{x}{2} } \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\cosh \frac{x}{2} }{\sinh \frac{x}{2} } \cdot \mathrm{sech}^{2} \frac{x}{2} \mathrm{d} \left( \frac{x}{2} \right)  \\ &=\int \frac{1}{\tanh \frac{x}{2} } \mathrm{d} \left( \tanh \frac{x}{2} \right)  \\ &=\ln \left\vert \tanh \frac{x}{2} \right\vert  +C\\ &=\ln \left| \frac{e^{\frac{x}{2} }-e^{-\frac{x}{2} }}{e^{\frac{x}{2} }+e^{-\frac{x}{2} }} \right|  +C    \end{align}$` 

还有

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\left( \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h}^{2} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\  \mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x}{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x\right)  }{\mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x} \\ &=\ln \left| \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x\right|  +C  \end{align}$` 

以上三种结果是等价的，因为

 `!$\displaystyle \frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{\cosh x-1}{\cosh x+1} \right\vert  =\frac{1}{2} \ln \left\vert \frac{2\sinh^{2} \frac{x}{2} }{2\cosh^{2} \frac{x}{2} } \right\vert  =\ln \left| \tanh \frac{x}{2} \right|   =\ln \left| \frac{e^{\frac{x}{2} }-e^{-\frac{x}{2} }}{e^{\frac{x}{2} }+e^{-\frac{x}{2} }} \right|  $` 

和

 `!$\displaystyle \ln \left| \mathrm{c} \mathrm{s} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x-\mathrm{c} \mathrm{o} \mathrm{t} \mathrm{h} \  x\right|  =\ln \left| \frac{1-\cosh x}{\sinh x} \right|  =\ln \left| \tanh \frac{x}{2} \right|   =\ln \left| \frac{e^{\frac{x}{2} }-e^{-\frac{x}{2} }}{e^{\frac{x}{2} }+e^{-\frac{x}{2} }} \right|  $` 

(35)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{sech}\ x$`  的原函数

 `!$\begin{align} \int \mathrm{s} \mathrm{e} \mathrm{c} \mathrm{h} \  x\mathrm{d} x &=\int \frac{1}{\cosh x} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=\int \frac{1}{\frac{e^{-x}+e^{x}}{2} } \mathrm{d} x\\ &=2\int \frac{1}{e^{-x}+e^{x}} \mathrm{d} x\\ &=2\int \frac{e^{x}\mathrm{d} x}{1+\left( e^{x}\right)^{2}  } \\ &=2\int \frac{\mathrm{d} \left( e^{x}\right)  }{1+\left( e^{x}\right)^{2}  } \\ &=2\arctan \left( e^{x}\right)  +C \end{align}$` 

(36)  `!$f\left(x\right)=\mathrm{coth}\ x$`  的原函数
